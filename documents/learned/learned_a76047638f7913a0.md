# Learned answer (user-corrected)

**Question:** no of test conditions

**Answer:** The `no_of_test_conditions` field represents the count of distinct experimental settings or environmental conditions (such as specific temperature, humidity, or other controlled factors) that were applied during the execution of a particular test.
