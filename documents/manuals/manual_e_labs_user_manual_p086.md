20.3 What is App Group Of Group And How To Create It?
Group of group means if two different groups may also belongs to any one group then you can create it here.

Figure: "APP GROUP OF GROUP" dialog window. It features the following fields: "Name" (with text "FINANCE GROUP OF GROUP"), "App Group List" (with a search icon and a "+" button), "App User" (with a search icon and a "+" button), and "App User Approver" (with a search icon and a "+" button). An "UPDATE" button is located in the bottom right corner.

* Name- This is the group of group name.
* App Group List- Add different group for who may also belongs to different groups.
* App User- Select users individually.
* App User Approver- Give name who will approve this group of group.

Then click on Save or Update according to your requirement.
