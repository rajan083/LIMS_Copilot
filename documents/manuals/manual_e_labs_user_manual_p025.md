6.1 How To Enter Details in Account?
The Account section is a company name and have all the details of clients who have their samples tested in the laboratory. To access, click on Account followed by Add button, then the following screen will appear.

Figure: ACCOUNT dialog window. Fields: Customer Name; Parent account; Address Line 1; Address Line 2; Land-line; Country; Tamil Nadu; City; Company Pincode; Customer Mobile; Customer Phone; Customer Email; Customer Type; Test Number; Sales Coordinator; Status; Credit Period; Button: Add; Button: Save.

• Customer Name – This is the client’s company name.
• Parent Name- If the Company has a parent Company, then fill in here.
• Address1 and Address2- Address of the company.
• Customer Mobile- Customer's mobile number.
• Customer Email- Customer's email id.
• Customer Type- For the customer type, choose either "Government" or "Private" accordingly from the dropdown menu.
• Sales Coordinator- Choose a sales coordinator who will be using this feature.
• Status – Select the status either as active or inactive as per your requirement.
• Credit Period- Fill in this option if you want to receive payment from the client at any specific time. That time will be mentioned here.
