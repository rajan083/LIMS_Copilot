17 Task Management?

Users can view the record of assigned tasks online whether they are created by you or assigned by a third party. For this first access the Task Management module.

Figure: Screenshot of the Task Management interface. Buttons: Search Module; Home; Support Task Master; Create Task; Assigned Tasks; Task Management Settings. Table columns: Action; Ticket Number; Project; Task Start Date; Priority; Summary; Assignee Name.

Here we have four menus: -
i) Support Task Master
ii) Create Task
iii) Assigned Tasks
iv) Task management settings.

17.1 Support Task Master?

If any customer faces any problems or bugs in their software, they can raise a ticket for this. Our software has two separate ways to access the support master.

Step 1: Go to Task Management and then click Support Task Master.
Step 2: Click on the profile icon and the sidebar appears on the right side of the screen. From that sidebar, click the Raise/View Ticket option.

Click on the Add button and a popup appears on the screen.
