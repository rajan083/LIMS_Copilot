1 Introduction

"LIMS" means Laboratory Information Management System. It is a software-based system designed to monitor and manage various aspects of laboratory operations, such as sample tracking, data management, workflow automation, and monitoring LIMS are commonly used in science and research facilities across industries, including pharmaceutical, biotechnology, environmental testing, forensic research, building material labs, automotive labs, chemical testing, civil testing, textile testing lab and research institutes. LIMS’ primary functions include specimen registration and tracking, test planning and selection, data capture and management, instrument integration, quality control and compliance management, results reporting, and data analysis LIMS help streamline laboratory processes, improve data accuracy, efficiency, and ensure compliance. It provides an on-site facility for collecting, organizing, and receiving laboratory data, and facilitates collaboration between scientists and engineers.

1.1 Who can benefit from E-Labs LIMS?

Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS) can help laboratories, scientists, engineers, quality control teams, data management and research teams, administrative staff, as well as clients and customers LIMS streamline laboratory operations, improve data integrity maintains, improves efficiency, ensures compliance, data analysis reporting and streamlines, optimizes -Enables product management and delivers timely and accurate results to customers.
