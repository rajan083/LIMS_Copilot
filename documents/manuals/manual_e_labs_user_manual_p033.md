7 Order Management Process?

When we receive any sample from a customer or lab representative for its testing it goes from eight stages. You can take ideas from the below image.

Figure: A horizontal process flow diagram with eight circles connected by lines, representing the order management workflow. The labels are: SAMPLE RECEIVING, BOOKING, SCHEDULING, TESTING, SECTION INCHARGE, REVIEWING, FINALIZING QA, APPROVAL, INVOICING, DISPATCHING.

The order management process flow goes from eight phases, shown in the below image.

Phase 1- Product Master Creation.
Phase 2- Sample Receiving.
Phase 3- Order Booking.
Phase 4- Schedule.
Phase 5- Testing.
Phase 6- Report.
Phase 7- Invoicing.
Phase 8- Dispatching.
