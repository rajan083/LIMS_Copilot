8.4 How Can I Allocate a Job to Analyst?

Step 1: Now for job allocation, click on Scheduling > Allocation. The list of parameters is already shown here. Now click on update icon.

Figure: Allocation dialog box showing fields for Client Name, Test Parameters, Priority, Code/Testing Days, Report Due Date, Reporting, Test Analyst, Equipment, and Remarks. Contains an Update button.

The above screen will appear. Here which parameter is going to be tested by its specialist the details can be filled here. Some of the details are already filled and the user must allocate a particular parameter to its specialist by selecting test analyst from its dropdown. Lastly, click on the Update button. Now this will be showing in dashboard of that specialist. This is the way users allocate parameters for its testing one by one to their analyst.

Users can also allocate bulk parameters. Click on Scheduling > Allocation and click on Add button. The following image will appear on your screen.

Figure: Bulk Allocation dialog box. Fields include Department, Group Name, Equipment (with options: Option: BOD incubator; Option: Chemically; Option: ISE), Test Analyst, Sample Name, and Test Parameter. Contains Add and Update buttons.
