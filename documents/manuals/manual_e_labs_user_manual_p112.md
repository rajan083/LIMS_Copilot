Once you click on the highlighted tick icon a popup will appear where assigned manager will have to give his remarks.
Figure: APPROVED CUSTOMER LIST(2) table with columns: Designation, Status (dropdown options: Satisfactory, Not Satisfactory), Comment By Sales Executive, Remark(Text box), Save button.
Your assigned senior has to provide the comment on the basis of the detailed filled by the concerning sales executive that he is satisfied with his travel report or travel visit. If he selects not satisfactory option then mention comment for reason. Also he can preview the expense claim which is submitted by the sales person. After clicking on update button status will be changed also it is now showing on the dashboard of finance.
Note: Once travel plan and travel report will approved by the concerning manager then only it is showing on the finance dashboard.
