This illustrates the available customization options for printing reports.

We have multiple options here for report printing.

Report Type
i) Print Report: By selecting this option, this will remove the header and footer of the company. This will print only reports without header and footer.
ii) Final Report: By selecting this option, this will print the report with the header and footer of the company.

Signature
i) Without Digital Signature: Through this choice, reports will be printed without the signature of the person in charge (reviewer, finalizer).
ii) With Digital Signature: Through this choice, reports will be printed with the digital signature of the person in charge (reviewer, finalizer).

Note: Signature of reviewer/finalizer is completely optional. This is dependent on your choice. We can customize accordingly.

Equipment
i) Without Equipment: This will print a report with the instrument details which are used for testing. So, this will show the instrument detail also.
ii) With Equipment: This will print reports without instrument details which are used for testing. This will not show this.
