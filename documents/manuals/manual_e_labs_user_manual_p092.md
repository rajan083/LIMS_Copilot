21.1 How To Generate E-TRF?
When you complete steps for creating enquiry that the customer wants testing for particular product against specific parameter. These are the same steps as mentioned in the CRM module. You can refer CRM module.

Once you create quotation against enquiry and after customer agreed on quotation you can create E-TRF (Electronic Test Request Form). Here Place New Quotation and New Quotation Letter are same which is already explained in CRM module.
