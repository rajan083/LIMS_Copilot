To create E-TRF go to this tab and click on Add button. A popup will appear.

Figure: Screenshot showing an E-Labs dashboard with a list of entries under columns: Action; Enquiry Number; Customer No; Branch; Department; Company Name; City; Sample Name; Cost Per Test. It shows an "Add" button and a data grid containing multiple rows of customer information.

In Customer Information section, select company name then the information regarding this customer will auto populated. In quotation letter drop down select quotation against this customer. Once you select the quotation you can see all the club quotations against this selected quotation. You can take idea from below image.
