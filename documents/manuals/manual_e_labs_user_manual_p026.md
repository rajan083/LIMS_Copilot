6.2 Create Contact?
We can create multiple contacts against a single company name.

Figure: CONTACT dialog window. Fields: Company Name; Company Unit; Contact Person First Name; Contact Person Last Name; Contact Email; Customer Mobile; Contact Phone; Contact Designation; Status; Button: Save.

Fill necessary details here like
• Contact Person First Name
• Contact Person Last Name
• Contact Email
• Customer Mobile
• Status

6.3 Enquiry Master?
Enquiries are generated by the sales executive of the lab. The details of the Sample, Parameter, Reference Test Standard, Customer and Contact person will be mentioned by the sales executive during the generation of the query. Whenever a customer has a query, his/her information should be stored in this section. For this, click on Enquiry master then click on Add button if it is a new enquiry.
