E-Labs

Figure: A flow chart titled "Order Management Process Flow" showing the lifecycle of a sample from "Start" to "End". 
Vertical axis categories: Product Master Creation, Sample Receiving, Order Booking, Testing, Report, Invoicing, Dispatching. 
Flow steps include: Receive sample with reference to TRF on reception; Move to order booking; Enter product and sample information, along with client contact to create a contact master; Auto generated sample receiving code in format DUMMY0001; SAMPLE REGISTRATION; An order is booked along with sample order detail & expected due date; Sample Status Tracking for Customers; Job Allocation by a Technical Person; Confirm order booking detail and print analytical sheet; Assign an analyst for sample testing based on parameters and methods required; Testing Performed by the Analyst; Section In-Charge Review Parameters Of Concerning Section; QA Approval; Report Finalization; Test Report Review no. will be generated; Invoice Generation; QA approved reports displayed on the invoice dashboard; PDF conversions of reports and auto mailing; Invoice reports displayed on dispatcher dashboard for dispatching purposes; AR NO; Docket no. of report and invoice will be entered in LIMS for future references; The reports and invoices are uploaded on the web portal to be tracked and downloaded.
