E-labs User Manual, page 113. Source file: E-labs User Manual.pdf.

If the finance person wants to approve expense claims, then go to Expense Approval menu under Approval tab.

Figure: Screenshot of the "Expense Approval" screen. Sidebar menu shows "Search Modules", "Quotation", "Customer Portal", "Finance", "Payment Release", "Expense Approval", "Master", "Order Management", and "Approval". The main table displays columns: "Action", "Employee Name", "Travel ID", "City", "Travel Date", "Return Date", "Claim Info", and "Status Of Finance". Rows show various employee entries with checkboxes and edit icons.

Now, click on edit icon which you want to update. After clicking, a popup screen will appear. You can see uploaded expense claim documents in travel report.

Figure: Screenshot of the "TRAVELING EXPENSES" popup dialog. Fields include "Travel Plan No.", "Employee Name", "Date", "Department", "Designation", "Travel Date", "Return Date", and "Remarks". A "Travel Claim" section includes columns: "Date", "Mode Of Travel", "Price In Km", "Distance In Km", "Local TA", "Travel Fare", "Dearness Allowance", "Food", "Hotel", "Parking", "Toll Charge", "Miscellaneous", "Summary", and "Total For The Day". A "TRAVEL CLAIM DOCUMENT" section includes columns: "Name", "Type", and "Action".

Step 1: Total Amount- This shows the total amount which you want to claim.
Step 2: Approved Amount- Finance person will have to enter the amount which he wants to approve.
