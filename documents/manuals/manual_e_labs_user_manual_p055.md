14 Where To Generate Invoice?

To generate invoices against the company we have to go to the invoicing module.

Figure: The Invoicing module screen showing a list of invoices with a sidebar containing menu items: Admin, Identity & Access Management, Invoicing, Sample Invoices, Credit Notes, Debit Notes. The main area shows a grid of orders with columns: Action, Invoice No, Branch, Department, Invoice Date, Company. This illustrates the location of the invoicing module.

We have multiple options here for report printing.

iii) Print Report- By selecting this option, this will remove the header and footer of the company. This will print only reports without header and footer.
