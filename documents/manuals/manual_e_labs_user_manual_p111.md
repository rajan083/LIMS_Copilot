E-labs User Manual, page 111. Source file: E-labs User Manual.pdf.

Figure: Travel Claim section. Fields: Travel Claim, Date, Mode Of Travel, Price In Km, Distance In Km, Local TA, Travel Fare, Dearness Allowance, Food, Hotel, Parking, Toll Charge, Miscellaneous, Summary, Total For The Day. Buttons: "SAVE/UPDATE CLAIM" and "TRAVEL CLAIM DOCUMENT". Plus icon for adding multiple days.
Here fill all the necessary details which you want to claim but updating travel claim document is necessary. Without updating Travel Claim Document system will not accept expense claim. If user wants to claim for multiple days then he/she can add from the + icon which is highlighted in the above image. For saving/updating the record click on Save/Update button as per your requirement.
In the similar manner of travel plan, travel report will go for approval to the assigned manager. On the dashboard of assigned manager the details will visible and to give remarks go to Travel Report Approval menu under Approval tab and then click on edit icon.
