Signature
iii) Without Digital Signature- Through this choice, reports will be printed without the signature of the person in charge (reviewer, QA approver, finalizer).
iv) With Digital Signature- Through this choice, reports will be printed with the digital signature of the person in charge (reviewer, QA approver, finalizer).

Note:- Signature of reviewer/ QA approver/ finalizer is completely optional. This is dependent on your choice. We can customize accordingly.

Equipment
iii) Without Equipment- This will print a report with the instrument details which are used for testing. So, this will show the instrument detail also.
iv) With Equipment- This will print reports without instrument details which are used for testing. This will not show this.

Limit
iii) Without Limit- This will print reports without showing required details.
iv) With Limit- This will print a report showing requirement details.

Accreditation
iii) Without Accreditation- By selecting this choice, this will print a report by giving you parameters which are false for the scope according to the NABL guidelines.
iv) With Accreditation- By selecting this choice, this will show parameters on the first page which are true for the scope according to the NABL guidelines. Then the parameters which are false will be shown after the true parameters in the report.
