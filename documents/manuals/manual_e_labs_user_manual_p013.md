5 Product Master?
Our system is designed in such a way that all the data is integrated with other modules from the product master. So, for this, we need to create the product master first before populating the data in other modules.
5.1 Product Category?
Now click on the Master module to enter the data into the product master and you will land on the product category menu.
Figure: Screenshot of the Product Category menu page, showing a sidebar with "Master" expanded and "Product Category" selected. The main view displays a table with columns: Edit icon, Name, Parent, Created By, Created On, Updated On, Updated By, and Status. The table contains entries like "Avg Bedding Sent 1", "Acetonit Rpe", "Ayurvedic Medicine", "Ayurvedic Testing", "Bakery & Confectionery Product", "Sweeting Gen", "Derma", "Complete Product", and "Face Makeup". There is a clickable button labeled "Add".
Click on the Add Button here and a popup screen will appear on your screen.
